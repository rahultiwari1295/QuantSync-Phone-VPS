AI enhance

Your rules stay yours. The AI just shows you a better version.

After a backtest, the AI looks at every trade your scan took and learns what the winners had in common. It tests the improved version on years it was never trained on, and only offers it if it still holds up.

  • Better entries. Filters out the matches that tended to fail, such as weak sectors or stocks under their long-term average.
  • Better exits. Tries many ways to close a trade, like trailing stops, targets and time limits, and keeps the one that held up best.
  • Plain-language reasons. You see what the AI learned, not a black box.
  • One tap back. Switch to the AI version or back to your original whenever you like.

Questions

Before you start

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You build a scan by picking an indicator, a comparison and a value, like “RSI(14) is below 35”. QuantSync writes it out in a plain sentence so you can check it reads the way you meant.

What data does it use?

Daily and weekly candles for every NSE-listed stock, updated after each market close. Backtests report 2023 to the latest close; the AI also learns from earlier years.

What exactly does the AI change?

Two things, only when you ask. For entries, it adds a filter that skips the kinds of matches that tended to fail. For exits, it picks the closing rule that held up best. You see the before and after, and nothing changes until you tap “Use this version”.

Will it tell me what to buy?

No. QuantSync Lab is a research and tracking tool. A match means a stock fit a scan’s rules. It is information, not advice.

What is a live record?

From the day you save a strategy, every new match is recorded as it happens. That record can’t be edited, so it shows how the strategy really behaves, separate from the backtest.
